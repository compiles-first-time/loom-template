Drag a cell to move it, drag the water to pan · Ctrl + scroll to zoom · Right-click a cell to paint it (long-press on touch).
Once Cell Multiplication is unlocked, cells divide about every 7 s and die at about 30 s — each leaves four behind, so the colony grows quickly. Before that your cell is immortal. Daughters are always blank white — every colour on your body is one you painted. Cells never pull on each other — they only push apart when they overlap — so they stay exactly where you put them and any shape you sculpt holds. Unattached cells drift past keeping their own colour; steer into one to absorb it. Traits are free to switch on and off — only levelling them costs. Cell multiplication can be switched off at any time — paused, nothing divides and nothing dies. Zoom out far enough to find the sea/air barrier and the beach east of it — crossing it needs at least 2 myocytes, 2 osteocytes and 2 pneumocytes, which gets you onto the beach — staying up in open air needs 5 pterocytes, or you simply fall. Paint a Ganglion and ctrl+right-click it to bind W, A, S or D; Q and E turn the organism and W A S D drive it relative to that heading — the cyan arrow shows which way is forward. More bound ganglia push harder. Z deletes the cell under the cursor and X drops a ganglion there — both work in any mode, up to the tissue cap. Press C for creative mode: every trait on and fully levelled, and cells pull on each other very slightly.
