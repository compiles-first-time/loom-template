## Known limits

- **Floor 3 (deterministic scripts for fragile steps) is an authoring rule, not a mechanical check** — a checker cannot know which steps are fragile. It lives in the EAC standard and is reviewed at HR registration.
- **The when-language regex is a heuristic.** It caught one real defect on first run; it will pass a description that says "when" vacuously. The floor is presence, not eloquence.
- **The ~35%/13% public-skill-audit figures are practitioner-corpus, uncorroborated** — labeled as such in L4. The vetting posture stands on LR-01 regardless.
