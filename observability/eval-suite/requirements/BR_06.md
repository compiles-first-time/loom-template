# BR_06 — Passive agent-reputation projection (ADR-0053 Step 1)

> **Requirement source:** handoff 2026-07-15 "Pending work" #4 → [ADR-0053](../../../adr/0053-agent-reputation-and-dispatch.md) **Step 1** (passive projection; Guardrail A). Panel dependency for the deliberation panel ([BR_07](./BR_07.md)).
> **Registry format:** [ADR-0046](../../../adr/0046-requirements-exceptions-testcase-registry.md) (extends [ADR-0022](../../../adr/0022-xlsx-docs-convention.md)).
> **Solution:** [`observatory/lib/reputation.mjs`](../../../observatory/lib/reputation.mjs) (pure projection + published formula) wired into [`observatory/lib/aggregator.mjs`](../../../observatory/lib/aggregator.mjs) `state.reputation`; accrual emitter [`scripts/lib/reputation.mjs`](../../../scripts/lib/reputation.mjs) (`emitReputationEvent`).
> **Validated by:** [`observatory/lib/reputation.test.mjs`](../../../observatory/lib/reputation.test.mjs) (asserts + emits these rows on every `node scripts/test.mjs`). Cases: [`BR_06.cases.mjs`](./BR_06.cases.mjs).
> **Live view:** Observatory → **Requirements** panel; reputation data in `state.reputation`.

The ask: **make each agent's track record visible as a transparent, published, confidence-smoothed score — projection ONLY.** Preferential dispatch (ADR-0053 Steps 2-3) is intentionally NOT built: a score that steers work without consent would be the fundamental wrong (Rule 2), so Steps 2-3 are gated on a `constitution-service` re-validation. Shipping the projection alone is Rule-safe and independently valuable (ADR-0053 §Fallback). Scores are re-derivable by any agent from its own visible counts (Rule 22), and small samples are smoothed toward a neutral prior so one failure can't tank a new agent (the 2026-07-07 quality-*rate* refinement).

## Known limitations (tracked, not hidden)

- **Emission points are incremental (SE, honest):** Step 1 ships the projection + formula + emitter. Wiring `emitReputationEvent` into every verifier gate (ADR-0044), critic approval, and lesson promotion is follow-on work; until then the panel is populated by whatever signals are emitted (plus passively-derived specialist invocations). The projection is correct for whatever it is fed; it does not fabricate signals.
- **Per-agent attribution (SE):** most mechanical events are session-scoped, not agent-scoped. The projection attributes only genuinely agent-scoped events (`reputation_event.agent`, `specialist_name`); it does not guess an agent for anonymous tool calls.
- **Steps 2-3 absent by design (BE-01):** consent/contestation/audit (Step 2) and preferential dispatch (Step 3) are out of scope and must be re-validated by `constitution-service` before shipping (ADR-0053 §Critical Constitutional Gate).
