## Known limits

- **Session-level, not per-agent.** Claude Code does not expose a subagent's own name to its hooks (see `provenance.mjs`), so adherence cannot currently be attributed to the specific agent that ignored a suggestion. Recorded rather than papered over.
- **The classifier is heuristic** (ADR-0017). A false-positive suggestion that is correctly declined still costs the operator a `skill_declined` event. This is the accepted price of the metric being honest about what was suggested.
