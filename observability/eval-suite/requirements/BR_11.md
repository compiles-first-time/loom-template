# BR_11 — Verifier gate → reputation emission (ADR-0044 + ADR-0053)

> **Requirement source:** whole-template map (2026-07-15) finding — `emitReputationEvent` was exported but NEVER called, so only the `invocation` kind populated the reputation projection. Implements the missing ADR-0044 verifier-gate call site feeding ADR-0053 Step 1.
> **Registry format:** [ADR-0046](../../../adr/0046-requirements-exceptions-testcase-registry.md).
> **Solution:** [`scripts/lib/verify-gate.mjs`](../../../scripts/lib/verify-gate.mjs) (`recordVerification` / `recordLessonContributed` / `recordCriticApproval`) wired into [`scripts/lib/specialist-lifecycle.mjs`](../../../scripts/lib/specialist-lifecycle.mjs) (`retire` → verifier_pass; `promote-lessons` → lesson_contributed); audited by [`observatory/lib/aggregator.mjs`](../../../observatory/lib/aggregator.mjs) `verifier_result` handler.
> **Validated by:** [`scripts/lib/verify-gate.test.mjs`](../../../scripts/lib/verify-gate.test.mjs) (6 cases; the BR case spawns the gate in a temp Loom root and reads back the emitted events). Cases: [`BR_11.cases.mjs`](./BR_11.cases.mjs).
> **Live view:** Observatory → **Reputation** panel (scores now move from real verifier signals) + activity feed (`verifier-pass`/`verifier-fail`).

The ask: **make the reputation projection accrue from REAL signals, not just specialist invocations.** ADR-0053 Step 1 shipped the projection + emitter, but nothing called the emitter — the score was populated only by the `specialist_spawned` side-channel. The verifier gate (ADR-0044) is the honest call site: when an agent's work is verified, record a typed pass/fail — it audit-logs a `verifier_result` and accrues the paired `reputation_event`. Still **projection-only** (Rule 2): recording a pass/fail changes visible track record, confers no dispatch preference (Steps 2-3 remain gated on constitution-service).

## Known limitations (tracked, not hidden)

- **Retire ⇒ verifier_pass is a proxy (SE, honest):** a specialist retiring implies it completed its assignment, but retirement is not itself a graded verifier. A stricter gate would record the actual eval outcome (ADR-0021/0044 `test_suite`/`human_gate`) at the point of grading; `retire` is the cleanest always-fires signal available today.
- **Base-agent signals still partial:** `critic_approval`/`constitution_check_passed` accrue only when `recordCriticApproval` (or the CLI) is called — the base agents' `claim`-event reviews are not yet auto-derived into reputation. Follow-on: a `claim`-handler derivation, or having the critic/constitution subagents call the gate.
- **Projection-only (Rule 2):** no dispatch preference is derived; Steps 2-3 gated on constitution-service.
