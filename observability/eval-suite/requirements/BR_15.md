> Register per [ADR-0046](../../../adr/0046-requirements-exceptions-testcase-registry.md),
> schema per [ADR-0061](../../../adr/0061-requirements-register-role-and-verifier-fields.md).

The ask: the Stop hook wrote a **descriptive** row (tool calls, errors, destructive ops). Description is not a grade. Nothing answered *"did this session hold the discipline?"* — the question ADR-0054 says Loom keeps failing silently. **No inference anywhere in the grader:** the thing that measures whether the model behaved must not itself be a model (`arXiv:2606.19544` — judges can be self-consistent and invalid).

## Known limits

- **A `fail` grade is advisory.** It is emitted at Stop and surfaced by doctor; it does not gate anything. Making it gate would require the decision to move to PreToolUse, which is a different ADR.
- **The 0.8 adherence floor and the 12-tool-call claim threshold are calibration guesses**, not measurements. Both should be re-tuned once enough `session_compliance` events exist to see the distribution.
